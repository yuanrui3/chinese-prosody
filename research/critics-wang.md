# Wang Zhi on sound and parallel prose

**Established in the source.** In the two juan of Wang Zhi’s (王銍) 《四六話》, Wang Tao (王陶) recommends two specific pairings on grounds of sound. The book also records competing wordings, praise for recitation and explicit literary preferences. Across the 58 entries screened, none identifies both an accepted and a rejected wording of one passage and assigns the preference to sound or cadence. This count describes the pinned transcription. [《四六話》, both juan and signed preface](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2098020)

## Two comparisons with surviving wordings

Letters A and B indicate witnesses. Each side has four, six, four and six characters in succession. Punctuation marks those divisions for the comparison. The forms below preserve each cited witness’s characters.

| I.5, Xia Song (夏竦), excusal from a diplomatic mission | 義不戴天，難下單于之拜；哀深陟屺，忍聞禁佅之音。 | 義不戴天，難下穹廬之拜；禮當枕塊，忍聞夷樂之聲。 | Both versions retain 拜 and change the final word from 音 to 聲. Xia’s collection preserves A as a letter (啓). Ouyang Xiu (歐陽修) quotes B as a memorial (表) and reports contemporary admiration for its parallelism. The documents may be distinct. [A, 《文荘集》卷十七](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=699715) · [A, 《四六法海》卷六](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=769829) · [B, 《歸田錄》](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2599511) |
| II.5, Liu Qi (劉跂), 《昭雪先公謝執政啓》 | 晚歳離騷，難招魂於鬼域；平生精爽，或見夢於故人。 | 晚歳離騷，魂竟招于異域；平生精爽，夢猶託于故人。 | The terminal characters 域 and 人 stay fixed while word order and diction change. A appears in Wang’s opening quotation. Wang also reports B’s wording from another copy, writing 於 where Liu’s collection has 于. He identifies the ghost appearing in a friend’s dream as an allusion to reburial. [A and the variant, 《四六話》卷下](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2098020) · [B, 《學易集》卷五](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=770542) |

Wang’s Xia entry illustrates the value of combining familiar and unfamiliar allusions. His quotations differ from both independently checked witnesses. Among other differences, he prints 陟岵 where Xia’s collected letter has 陟屺, and his quotation of Ouyang has 情深陟岵 where Ouyang’s own book has 禮當枕塊. Wang’s explicitly favorable comparison applies to **another** couplet in the Xia piece, beginning 王姬築館. Neither comparison above carries a critic’s vote on relative 音律美. ## Coverage and interpretation

The screen covered the signed preface, 28 entries in the upper juan and 30 in the lower juan. Entry numbers here follow the paragraphs of the pinned edition. The later imperial catalogue notice was excluded from the author’s judgments. The Gilbreth run stores the edition manifest with revision IDs and SHA-256 hashes, the 58-entry exclusion ledger with identifying text spans, and the checked neutral pairs under `editions/wang/{manifest.jsonl,screen.json,neutral-pairs.json}`. These are digital witnesses. This audit does not establish a manuscript stemma or verify the reading of every glyph against a facsimile. [Pinned edition](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2098020)

**Project hypothesis.** The Xia and Liu examples can test whether an evaluator or a synthetic voice responds differently to alternative diction at fixed length. Their evidence supports neither an aesthetic winner nor a judgment of ease of oral production (顺口).
