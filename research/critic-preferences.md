# Can the written scorer recover a critic's preference about sound?

Screen 王銍’s 《四六話》, 謝伋’s 《四六談麈》 and 孫梅’s 《四六叢話》. Start from the critics and their stated reasons before opening model predictions. Treat attributed anecdotes as reports, preserve disputed readings and identify repeated quotations from the same family. Record author, genre, period, source revision or scan page, both exact spans if available, and the basis and direction of the judgment. Verify quotations against the work or another primary witness where possible. Record unresolved leads under UNVERIFIED.

**Primary endpoint.** A scored preference needs two recoverable wordings, an identifiable direction and a reason explicitly tied to sound, phrasing, cadence or rhyme. Report Six Dynasties and Tang 骈文 and Song material separately. Tabulate later or out-of-genre material by its own period and genre. General precepts, examples of a recommended pairing, changes made for meaning, whole-work praise and unranked variants have separate entries. Never infer a low score from a critic's silence. A documented preference supplies an order only. Leave both 1–7 reference scores missing.

**Forecasts.** Freeze inputs, evidence classifications and acoustic hypotheses before looking at comparative results. Score variants as the same kind of unit in identical available context. A quotation without recoverable common neighbors is scored in isolation, with full context enabled within the quotation itself. Preserve clause delimiters and normalize comparison punctuation symmetrically. Replay the 291-row Yu Xin checkpoint before forecasting. Fit a one-variable least-squares regression of the earlier 337 editorial scores on total Han count, clipped to the original scale. Always giving 6 ties every pair. Count ties as half. When several comparisons descend from the same anecdote, average within family before averaging families. Report the actual denominator and coverage. Resolve known previously used cases, author or work overlaps, and documented direction before crediting held-out agreement. Keep the previously used 《峴山亭記》 passage as a calibration check. Report independently documented literary choices with a different or unspecified rationale only as a secondary test.

**Acoustic comparison.** Use the existing controlled Standard Mandarin voices zm_010, zf_001 and zf_002. For attested, reproducible wordings use the same punctuation scheme, nominal speed 0.9, within-family voice embedding and seed. Supply performed-reading targets, including proper names, separately from historical readings. Across an expansion, predict a longer elapsed span for the clause gaining characters. For a same-length change, record the observed differences without choosing a sign in retrospect. Use waveform-derived Mandarin phone boundaries for matched syllables and the project noise-aware detector for internal pauses. Missing links or unmatched pauses stay missing. Device repeat and word-order controls are technical checks. Treat decoder times as estimates and store WAVs, diagnostics and individual candidate readings on Gilbreth. No listener or read-aloud participant is involved. These tests do not measure 顺口.
