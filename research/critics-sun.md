# What Sun Mei’s 《四六叢話》 preserves about sound

**Established in the sources.** A sixth-century author’s preference survives for two ways of pronouncing the same character in the same passage. Shen Yue (沈約) approved Wang Yun’s (王筠) reading of 霓 in 《郊居賦》. The biographies specify both readings. A Song court anecdote independently names two drafts of a ceremonial address and gives a reason for preferring the easier one to deliver. It quotes neither draft. Neither case supplies two differently worded passages of parallel prose with a phrase-level musical verdict. [《梁書》卷三十三](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=762672) · [張端義《貴耳集》卷下](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=793294)

## One word, two readings

The 《梁書》 biography of Wang Yun, in the 四庫 witness, gives **雌霓連踡**. It glosses Wang’s pronunciation of 霓 as **五激反** and the pronunciation Shen feared as **五雞反**. The 《南史》 witness gives 五的反 and 五兮反 respectively. These are two accounts of one reading, with an entering tone (入聲) preferred over a level tone (平聲) at this occurrence. In his biography of Fan Zhen (范鎮), Sima Guang (司馬光) explicitly interprets Shen’s choice as a matter of local euphony. He objects to examiners rejecting the level reading in Fan’s different poem. The later 《廣韻》 registers the level reading and refers to two alternatives, including an entering reading. Its fanqie spellings are an independent lexicographic witness. [《梁書》王筠傳](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=762672) · [《南史》王筠傳](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=1477957) · [司馬光《范景仁傳》](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=706025) · [《廣韻》卷一](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2599423) · [《廣韻》三讀](https://ytenx.org/zim?dzih=%E8%9C%BA&dzyen=1&jtdt=1&jtkb=1)

Sun’s juan 26 reproduces Wang Mao’s (王楙) account in 《野客叢書》. That witness supplies the complete clauses **駕雌霓之連蜷，泛大江之悠永**. The fu in Shen’s own 《梁書》 biography reads **駕雌蜺之連卷，泛天江之悠永**. The differences extend to 大／天. A controlled reading test must hold one witness fixed on both sides. The four characters in Wang Yun’s biography isolate the documented preference directly. In the four-character biographical quotation 霓 is second. In the six-character clause beginning 駕 it is third. A written-input-only model sees identical characters. Any acoustic test would need a declared historical realization or an explicitly modern substitute. [《野客叢書》卷十五](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=639591) · [《郊居賦》 in 《梁書》卷十三](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=1494993) · [Sun, fascicle 3, PDF p. 109, printed p. 459](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC511-023031404038497-40990_四六叢話_附選詩叢話_（三）.pdf?page=109)

Sun also makes judgments in his own voice. His opening discussion of the ode (頌) in juan 16 questions whether Wang Bao’s (王褒) composition fits the form musically and praises the sound of Yuan Jie’s (元結) 《中興頌》. These remarks concern different compositions and purposes. They supply neither ranked renditions nor a controlled revision. In juan 28 the advice attributed to the Donglai master (東萊先生) favors clear, resonant wording even at the cost of verbal ingenuity. It gives a design principle without providing a particular rejected and accepted line. [《四六叢話》卷十六](https://www.shidianguji.com/book/CADAL02110211/chapter/1lcn2rburpsma) · [卷二十八](https://www.shidianguji.com/book/CADAL02110211/chapter/1lcn2s4z8h9v8)

## Scope and retrieval limits

The search covered images of all 33 numbered juan in the four public-domain National Library PDF fascicles. Gilbreth Slurm job `12540350` ran Tesseract 5.5.4 with traditional vertical Chinese on **686 pages**, including front matter and the separate appendix 《選詩叢話》. All 686 pages completed and 669 returned characters. The appendix contributes no prose case here. Median per-page OCR confidence by fascicle was 44, 43, 41 and 45 on Tesseract’s scale. Those numbers describe engine confidence, not transcription accuracy. We also retained manifests for 19 CADAL fascicles. [National Library fascicle 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC511-023031404038766-40848_四六叢話_附選詩叢話_一.pdf) · [CADAL scan catalogue](https://ctext.org/library.pl?res=4751)

The reproducible lexical screen paired a sound or pronunciation marker such as 聲律, 入聲, 讀作 or 押韻 with a preference or correction marker such as 不若, 改作 or 不如 within 90 OCR characters on the same page. Nineteen pages had a sound marker and 127 had a comparison marker. The proximity rule found just one page, a ranking of works in the discussion of 楚辭. It missed even the page with Shen’s independently verified choice. A second pass examined public indexed chapter excerpts from 31 juan, including candidate comparison language across OCR line breaks. Juan 17 and juan 24 had only the scan-based pass in that ledger. The second pass led to the reading, court and lyric cases above. The strongest leads were checked against the printed pages or earlier named witnesses. These are retrieval results. They do not establish how often sound preferences occur in the book.

The Gilbreth edition directory `editions/sun/` holds the four source PDFs with hashes and rights metadata, page-level OCR and screen results, the chapter coverage ledger, and pinned earlier witnesses. **UNVERIFIED** for this benchmark are independent full-text witnesses of both Yang and You drafts, Hao’s complete fu, and Teng’s complete fu. The quoted comparison passages identified above have direct witnesses of their own.
