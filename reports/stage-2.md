# Stage 2 · Mathematical foundations

The [foundations](../theory/foundations.md) define the representation, formal metrics, acoustic measures, and validation design. Each measure includes a worked example and its scope. The text tier uses historical tone and rhyme. The audio tier records the actual pronunciation of each take. 《广韵》 is the initial documentary reference for 骈文, with 平水韵 used for sensitivity checks and designated verse comparisons. The text retains unresolved readings and edition variants.

**Established source evidence.** 章惇’s reported preference in 《岘山亭记》 gives two versions of one passage. The historical labels are opposed at both pairs of endings. The ordered sequence changes from 平仄 to 仄平 alongside the change from four-character to six-character clauses. 《古文评注》 identifies a specific break to test. 莫道才’s comments on 《滕王阁序》 give a work-level hypothesis about the cadence of parallel clauses. [《默记》卷下](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/CZBZZ051/chapter/1kxs7vmwg6230) · [《岘山亭记》 and annotations](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/峴山亭記) · [莫道才](https://news.gxnu.edu.cn/2025/0802/c1330a319711/page.psp)

**Contested scope.** Treat ordered final tones and the proposed 马蹄韵 chain as measurable hypotheses for 骈文. For verse, track edition, rule source, and the exact definition of strict or relaxed compliance.

**Project hypotheses.** Test cold-reading difficulty and blind audio preference on matched passages with separate outcomes. Use independent manipulation checks, same-text recordings by multiple readers, and lexical and syntactic baselines. The English precedents motivate those designs. Estimate the effect of the historical revision as a whole before testing length or tone order with controlled edits. Validate on held-out works and readers. [Breen](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2018.01.014) · [Miller and Carter-Ényì](https://doi.org/10.1080/23268263.2018.1438963) · [Scheepers et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0074986)

A [six-excerpt author trial](../theory/foundations.md#appendix-six-excerpts-for-an-author-trial) checks the rating questions for 音律美 and 顺口. Stage 3 starts with a rights-documented, edition-pinned 骈文 pilot and matched prose. The numerical examples illustrate the formulas. Empirical model fitting, human data collection, and the composite score depend on later stages. Gilbreth remains the planned site for bulk data and GPU processing.

## Author trial response

One author read the six excerpts aloud in 普通话 in a fixed order and used both 1–7 scales. The scores fell between 5 and 7. 顺口 exceeded 音律美 by one point on three excerpts, and the two scores tied on three. This helped check how the author used the two scales. Individual ratings and the variety record are archived privately on Gilbreth. Evaluation of the measures will use independent readers and listeners and passages designed to span a wider range of ratings.
