# Annotated literature on 顺口 and 音律美

Focused bilingual review, 21 November 2026. The research treats 顺口 as ease of saying a passage and pleasure in hearing it, both relevant to the sonic and rhythmic qualities studied under 音律美. The annotations distinguish original texts and full studies, accessible author summaries, documented data resources, and unresolved leads.

## 1. Traditional prosodic theory

- **Shen Yue (沈約). “Commentary on the biography of Xie Lingyun” (《宋書·謝靈運傳論》), in 《宋書》, juan 67.** **Established textual evidence.** Shen proposes contrast among neighboring sounds and between paired lines, describing alternation with musical and perceptual analogies. His method is historical criticism and comparison of exemplary earlier writing, including Cao Zhi, Wang Can, Liu Kun and Lu Ji. It motivates local and cross-line contrast features for 順口. Phrases such as 浮聲 and 切響 require interpretation before coding, and the commentary provides neither an operational acoustic definition nor a complete eight-defect inventory. [Classical text](https://m.gushiwen.cn/shiwenv_310919cc61b1.aspx)

- **Liu Xie (劉勰). 《文心雕龍》, chapters 33 “Sound regulation” (聲律) and 35 “Parallel diction” (麗辭).** **Established textual evidence.** In 聲律 Liu locates sound regulation in the human voice and identifies certain separated shared-initial (雙聲) and shared-rime (疊韻) configurations with stumbling articulation. In 麗辭 he analyzes four types of pairing (言對、事對、反對、正對), using examples from sources including Sima Xiangru’s 《上林賦》 and Wang Can’s 《登樓賦》. The chapters develop their arguments through selected examples. They motivate separately scoring onset and final recurrence, and comparing the syntax and meaning of parallel clauses. The sound terms and transmission variants require philological decisions, and rhetorical rankings supply hypotheses for modern speech experiments. [聲律](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/文心雕龍/聲律) · [麗辭](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/文心雕龍/麗辭)

- **Kūkai (空海), compiler. 《文鏡秘府論》, west fascicle (西卷), “論病” and “文二十八種病.”** **Established textual evidence with competing definitions.** The compilation collects 28 faults, gives named alternatives, and demonstrates the first eight with verse examples. It preserves distinct interpretations of position comparisons such as 平頭 and 蜂腰, and recommendations differing by distance or adjacency for sound recurrences such as 小韻 and 傍紐. Examples are predominantly five-character verse, with a limited extension to other forms made explicit in some entries. Encode individual definitions with their provenance, and compute sensitivity to variant rules. The compilation is evidence that rules and disagreements circulated. It cannot establish uniform observance or ascribe the entire list to Shen Yue as a single authorial system. [West fascicle](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/文鏡秘府論#西卷)

- **Wang Yiqing (王奕清) et al., compilers. 《御定詞譜》 (also 《欽定詞譜》), Wenyuan Ge 《四庫全書》 edition, “凡例” and juan 1.** **Established editorial practice.** The compilers compare examples under each tune, mark whole and partial phrases (句、讀), allow both tone classes where exemplars are attested, and identify some positions requiring a particular tone such as 去. In juan 1, 《歸字謠》 (also 十六字令) is presented with 16 characters, four phrases, three level rhymes and specific flexible positions supported by alternate texts. This supplies a worked source for encoding variants and flexible slots in a tune-specific grammar. The Qing reconstruction describes written exemplars. Melody and modern perception require separate evidence. [凡例](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/御定詞譜_(四庫全書本)/凡例) · [卷01](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/御定詞譜_(四庫全書本)/卷01)

- **Zhou Deqing (周德清). 1324. 《中原音韻》, Wenyuan Ge 《四庫全書》 edition, lower fascicle, “正語作詞起例.”** **Established evidence for a northern 曲 convention.** Zhou assembles rhyme categories and uses examples from earlier works and pairs of character readings to illustrate compositional decisions. He explicitly links reassigning entering-category characters to 平、上、去 with extending the rhymes available to writers, and distinguishes speech, where he says an entering distinction remains. Use distinct fields for historical 入聲, 曲 rhyme assignment and spoken realization. Zhou’s prescription and retrospective examples cannot settle the phonetic value or geographic reach of the distinction. [卷下](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/SK3469/chapter/1lm3lpuuyd0yz)

- **Wang Jide (王驥德). Ming dynasty. 《曲律》, juan 2, section on level and oblique tones.** **Established evidence for a regional distinction in a treatise.** Wang argues from rhyme books, tune practice and vocal examples that southern 曲 retains a usable entering category whereas northern 曲 assigns its items to the other tones. He describes different treatment of the component oblique tones and attributes bad combinations to awkward singing (拗嗓). This links phonological categories with a genre-specific production hypothesis and argues for separate 北曲 and 南曲 profiles. The material is prescriptive and supplies neither measured pitch contours nor dated recordings. [卷第二](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/CZH124/chapter/1kxsj78rnn5t2)

- **Li Huei-mian (李惠綿). 2016. “評議周德清建構北曲曲牌格律之要素.” 《臺大中文學報》 54, 101–158.** **Established documentary analysis within the materials studied.** Li analyzes the rhyming manual, Zhou’s 40 commented exemplars under 定格 and subsequent northern tune manuals. The comparison shows that distinguishing core text (正字) from added syllables (襯字) can change the inferred phrase length. Li also identifies singing-related cues, such as 務頭 and lexical 陰陽 placement, that cannot be reliably recovered from later manuals. This supports modeling inserted syllables separately from tone-class flexibility and testing any performance feature against audio. The historical analysis depends on extant texts and partial descriptions of performance. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.6281/NTUCL.2016.09.54.03) · [Full text](https://www.cl.ntu.edu.tw/uploads/root/臺大中文學報/第五十四期/評議周德清建構北曲曲牌格律之要素.pdf)

- **Li Huei-mian (李惠綿). 2017. “從「入派三聲」說到「入代平聲」、「入代三聲」——論入聲字在填詞度曲中之原則與通變.” 《清華中文學報》 18, 149–207.** **Established classification in the manual and contested speech reconstruction.** Li compares Zhou’s explicit rules, examples of mixed rhyming in Yuan works and subsequent singing commentaries, including Shen Chongsui and Xu Dachun. Li interprets the sources as supporting a weakened spoken entering distinction and a separate northern operatic rhyme convention. This matters when coding historically entering syllables and comparing performed realizations across genres. Later 崑腔 singing practices provide evidence for their own period, and the original Yuan articulation remains uncertain. [Full text](https://cl.site.nthu.edu.tw/var/file/401/1401/img/1473/961109250.pdf)

- **Wang Zhi (王銍). Song dynasty. 《四六話》, juan 1–2, 《四庫全書》 transcription.** **Established textual evidence.** Wang’s criticism documents how specific Song court texts achieve effective doublets through both balanced wording and paired allusions. For example, his juan 1 account of Yuan Houzhi (元厚之) unpacks allusive matter in a memorial using Li Deyu’s 《獻替記》. It gives candidates for evaluating semantic and syntactic alignment in 骈文 independently of phonology. This is selected court writing discussed by a critic, and no distribution of typical clause-final tones can be inferred from it. [Text](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/四六話)

- **Sun Deqian (孫德謙). 1923. “六朝麗指序.” 《學衡》 23.** **Established authorial statement, preface only.** Sun presents Six Dynasties parallel diction as the exemplary case and explains that the book uses fine-grained literary criticism to examine features including expressive pacing, authorial style and the use of figures. The primary evidence reviewed here is the signed preface. It gives historical motivation for looking beyond clause length and tone to rhetorical organization. The preface offers no dataset, and the rest of the treatise still needs verification. [Issue facsimile](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/ba/NLC404-01J003295-32391_學衡_1923年23期.pdf)

- **Mo Daocai (莫道才). 2025. “骈文与韵文的关系.” 《光明日报》, July 28, p. 13.** **Contested genre interpretation and named critical judgment.** Mo argues for treating 骈文 as 韵文, citing tonal phrasing, recurring four- and six-character clauses, and rhyme. He describes reciting 王勃’s 《滕王阁序》 as an exemplary experience of rhythmic rise, fall, and articulation (抑扬顿挫), attributing it to alternation within clauses and tonal opposition across paired clauses. This is a work-level assessment with a testable explanation. The essay uses selected examples and literary criticism. It supplies no passage-specific listener observations. [Author’s university reprint](https://news.gxnu.edu.cn/2025/0802/c1330a319711/page.psp)

- **Tsai Yu (蔡瑜). 2019. “唐詩格律發展的關鍵進程——以日本詩格《作文大躰》為核心的探討.” 《政大中文學報》 31, 219–250.** **Established corpus result within the anthology sample. Historical dating is provisional.** Tsai compares the Japanese 詩格 manual with 《文鏡秘府論》 and tests specific rules on 1,774 five-character poems across 17 anthologies. Table 1 first computes per-poem applicability ratios and then averages them. Two versions of avoiding 上尾 yield different reported compliance (92.8% when compared as 平/仄, 96.9% for the four categories), and the result varies by collection. The paper argues for incremental development from the second-position rule (拈二) toward later 粘對 and tonal templates, with some earlier rules persisting as independently measurable preferences. That makes it a reproducible historical benchmark target with explicit denominators. Anthology selection, the identification of the transmitted rules, and uncertain dating of their composition limit historical inference, and the study uses text evidence. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.30407/BDCL.201906_(31).0006) · [Full text](https://bdcl.nccu.edu.tw/uploads/chapter_file/file/649a3c1136737600804feee1/唐詩格律發展的關鍵進程__以日本詩格_作文大__為核心的探討.pdf)

- **Dung Chau Hung (董就雄). 2015. “試論唐代八韻試賦的用韻.” 《饒宗頤國學院院刊》 2, 239–275.** **Established corpus finding for the source population.** Dung locates the extant Tang examination compositions listed by Peng Hongwei in 《歷代賦彙》 and analyzes the assigned and realized rhyme order. Of 139 surviving examination 賦, 101 use eight rhymes. Of the 97 eight-rhyme pieces with documented assignments, 66 were assigned four level and four oblique rhymes. Three patterns that alternate tone class within the two four-rhyme blocks account for about 82% of the realized sequences in that subset. This supplies a reproduction target for regulated 賦 (律賦) and demonstrates the value of distinguishing a prompt's rhyme order from the author's order. Preservation and examination selection limit generalization to other 賦. The author's account of periods and aesthetically optimal patterns is historical interpretation. [Journal full text](https://jas.hkbu.edu.hk/content/dam/jas-assets/publications/bulletin-of-the-jao-tsung-i-academy-of-sinology/issue-2-may-2015/10.pdf)

- **Chen Guo (陈果). 2020. “骈文界定问题的再审视.” 《成都理工大学学报（社会科学版）》 28(2), 77–81.** **Contested genre definition.** Chen compares competing definitions with historical criticism, anthology selections, and examples of matched and unmatched phrases. He treats pairing (对偶) as the defining feature and proposes a threshold of more than half the clauses in strict parallels, with sound regulation (声律) and four/six-character phrasing as variable. This motivates independently annotating source genre, pair status, and clause shape. Using a parallelism threshold to assign the genre would make a validation of that same feature circular. The threshold is a proposal without a labeled validation sample. [Journal full text](https://xbshkx.cdut.edu.cn/cn/article/pdf/preview/10.3969/j.issn.1672-0539.2020.02.013.pdf)

- **Zhai Jingyun (翟景运) and Mou Yanhong (牟艳红). 2012. “论唐代骈文文体观的变化.” 《东方论坛》 2012(2), 86–89.** **Established documentary evidence with historical interpretation.** The authors compare literary criticism and works from early, middle and late Tang, documenting changes in the status of 骈文 and its relationship with ancient-style prose (古文). They discuss how 《文选》 privileges paired commentary and encomia over unpaired biographical narrative. For a known-groups comparison, genre and period can correlate with document function and anthology selection. The sample is selective and the history concerns literary status and practice. It supplies no acoustic or clause-level frequency series. [Journal full text](https://dflt.qdu.edu.cn/__local/C/88/EC/58C4514309EEA472CE2D7EEE470_E87A5930_2AB2B.pdf)

- **Qigong (启功). “启功口述历史,” section “学艺回顾·学术著作篇（上）.” Beijing Normal University web edition, 25 May 2016.** **Established description of the author’s proposal.** In the oral account, Qigong describes the “bamboo pole” model (竹竿) from 《诗文声律论稿》 and derives four five-character line forms from windows of the repeating sequence 平平仄仄. He explains the seven-character extension, initial-position flexibility, and how an altered opening can leave an isolated level syllable (孤平). His method is demonstration on conventional patterns, with rhyme placement and 粘对 added at poem level. The four window examples are enough to anchor a formal generator. Claims about acoustic pleasantness and the model’s wider coverage require empirical tests, and the original book remains to check. [Author’s oral account, university edition](https://xcb.bnu.edu.cn/xywh/jsff/14401.html)

### Literary reception and passage-level criticism

- **Wang Zhi (王铚). 《默记》, lower fascicle, entry beginning 章子厚少年未改官.** **Attributed historical comparison.** Wang reports that 章惇 regarded the paired four-character clauses in 欧阳修’s 《岘山亭记》 as abrupt (突兀), and proposed adding 之上 and 之渊 to produce fitting cadence (中节). The received text contains the expanded pair. This is a documented preference between two versions of the same passage, with wording, clause length, and final sounds changing together. The same work in 《古文评注》 marks 文气一顿 after 皆足以垂于不朽, supplying a site for testing pause placement and lengthening. Treat rhetorical phrasing as a hypothesis for performed phrasing. [《默记》卷下](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/CZBZZ051/chapter/1kxs7vmwg6230) · [Received text and commentary](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/峴山亭記)
- **Zhang Duanyi (张端义). 《贵耳集》, juan 1, entry beginning 易安居士李氏.** **Historical critical judgment.** Zhang praises the fourteen-character reduplicated opening of 李清照’s 《声声慢》 and the unforced effect of 点点滴滴, and singles out 黑 as an unusual rhyme. On her 《永遇乐》 he also credits the fitting of ordinary diction to its tune. The remarks identify anchors for testing the pacing, grouping, and expressive effects of repetition. Their interpretive and tune-specific details belong to the historical context. [Original text](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/貴耳集)
- **Zhou Ji (周济). 《介存斋论词杂著》, entry beginning 双声叠韵字.** **Historical critical judgment.** Zhou identifies the final six characters of the same 《声声慢》 opening as deliberately patterned in shared initials (双声) and rimes (叠韵). His phrasing and counts are an additional critic’s reading of a particular written witness, with no assumption of independence from earlier commentary. The passage motivates exact-span tests of repeated segmental patterns. [Original text](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/介存齋論詞雜著)
- **Shen Yifu (沈义父). 《乐府指迷》, “词中去声字最紧要.”** **Historical negative case for singing.** Shen singles out 金 in a line set to 《尾犯》 as occupying a slot he says should take 去声 and reports that such cases impeded singers. The annotation identifies a position, a comparison category, and a reported difficulty. The claim is about tune setting in a historical singing practice. [Original text](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/樂府指迷#詞中去聲字最緊要)
- **Ouyang Xiu (欧阳修). 《六一诗话》, entry beginning 退之笔力.** **Historical comparative judgment.** Ouyang praises 韩愈’s 《此日足可惜》 for ranging among neighboring rhyme groups, and 《病中赠张十八》 for inventiveness within a narrow rhyme group. The remarks supply two admired rhyme strategies and make difficulty an explicit part of the second work’s appeal. They evaluate the poet’s craft at work level. [Original text](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/六一詩話)

## 2. Generative and phonological metrics

- **Established corpus finding.** San Duanmu and Nathan Stiennon. 2005. “Tonal Patterns in Chinese Regulated Verse: A Corpus Study.” *Taiwan Journal of Linguistics* 3(1), 1–32 (journal open-access index). DOI 10.6519/TJL.2005.3(1).1. The study compares counts of level and oblique (平仄) positions against strict and relaxed templates in Qiu Xieyou’s edition of *Three Hundred Tang Poems* (唐詩三百首). Its sample includes 114 seven-syllable poems and 672 lines, of which 54 poems are eight-line regulated verse (七言律詩) and 60 are quatrains (七言絕句). The abstract reports about 32% of lines and 1% of poems matching strict patterns, and 95% and 68%, respectively, with relaxed rules. In the author manuscript, table (23) gives 610/672 (90.8%) relaxed standalone line matches using default pronunciations, table (31) gives 584/672 (86.9%) matches with the expected line type fixed from the poem and alternate pronunciations allowed, and table (30) gives 20/54 (37.0%) relaxed matches for all eight lines of a poem in the eight-line subset. Tables (29) and (30) together yield 57/114 complete relaxed matches (50%) under that rule, so these body counts do not reconstruct the abstract’s headline percentages. Replication should distinguish standalone and whole-poem scoring, document alternate readings and editions, and compare matching denominators. Line type and position matter even within the relaxed first–third–fifth rule (一三五不論). The counts characterize a selected anthology on paper and provide no reading-aloud judgments. [Journal index](https://toaj.stpi.niar.org.tw/index/journal/volume/article/4b1141f983eb49dc0183f05012b40329) · [Author manuscript](https://public.websites.umich.edu/~duanmu/PingZe05.pdf)
- **Established corpus finding and contested analysis.** San Duanmu (端木三). 2004. “A Corpus Study of Chinese Regulated Verse: Phrasal Stress and the Analysis of Variability.” *Phonology* 21(1), 43–89. DOI 10.1017/S0952675704000132. From the same Qiu edition, Duanmu tags word categories and syntactic trees for 231 poems (788 five-syllable and 672 seven-syllable lines), tests the fit between syntax and an ideal tree, and proposes an alternative analysis based on phrasal stress maxima. He finds that some well-formed trees are rare and that raw frequency does not reliably track proposed metrical tension. This motivates controlling for syntactic and lexical opportunity before reading weights as how strongly poets avoided a pattern. The stress analysis depends on author-assigned parses and phonological premises. The corpus overlaps with Duanmu and Stiennon, so these are complementary analyses of substantially shared text. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0952675704000132) · [Author manuscript](https://public.websites.umich.edu/~duanmu/POETRY04.pdf)
- **Contested formal analysis.** Donna Jo Napoli. 2007. “The Tonal System of Chinese Regulated Verse.” *Annali Online di Ferrara – Lettere*, vol. 2, 1–42. Using five- and seven-syllable templates and examples from prior analyses, Napoli compares metrical trees, grids, and linear descriptions. She proposes retaining tree structure for rhythm and a linear system for tone, with the half-line as an organizing unit. This gives a reason to estimate tone-conformity and phrase-structure features independently, then test whether present-day performances align them. It is a comparative theoretical analysis without a new speaker or listener sample. [Journal full text](https://annali.unife.it/lettere/article/download/126/77)
- **Contested historical account.** Sheng-li Feng (馮勝利). 2011. “A Prosodic Explanation for Chinese Poetic Evolution.” *Tsing Hua Journal of Chinese Studies*, new series 41(2), 223–258 as paginated in the article. Close scansion of examples in the *Book of Odes* (詩經), *Li Sao* (離騷), related *Songs of Chu* (楚辭), and later examples motivates different models for double-foot verse (二步律) and pause-centered verse (頓歎律). Feng proposes that foot structure helps explain the appearance of fixed odd syllable counts. The article supports testing alternatives to a single fixed foot template. Its analyses are based on textual examples and reconstructed prosodic structure. Neither actual historical pauses nor present-day preferences are observed. [Journal full text](https://thjcs.site.nthu.edu.tw/var/file/452/1452/img/1294/THJCS412-1.pdf)
- **Established methodology outside Chinese verse.** Bruce Hayes and Colin Wilson. 2008. “A Maximum Entropy Model of Phonotactics and Phonotactic Learning.” *Linguistic Inquiry* 39(3), 379–440. DOI 10.1162/ling.2008.39.3.379. The authors fit weights on violations in a probability model trained on attested forms and compare its generalizations with data, including English onset judgments, Shona vowel harmony, stress patterns, and Wargamay phonotactics. Its method motivates regularized, interpretable estimates of poetic constraint weights. Transfer to verse requires an explicit candidate set, genre-conditioned evaluation and audio validation. Fitted weights depend on the offered candidates and training distribution. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/ling.2008.39.3.379) · [Author manuscript](https://www.brucehayes.org/Phonotactics/HayesWilsonMaximumEntropyPhonotacticsAugust2007.pdf)

- **Established corpus analysis, with proposed scansion.** Hsiao, Yu-chau (蕭宇超). 2010. “從語料庫觀點探討古詩節律” (“The Metrics of Gu-shi: A Corpus-Based Approach”). 《華語文教學研究》 7(1), 1–25. Hsiao annotates 1,735 lines from 89 old-style poems (古体诗) and Music Bureau (乐府) poems in the same Qiu Xieyou edition used by Duanmu. Tags include tone, foot structure, POS, syntax and proposed stress. The study models interactions between metrical and grammatical stress. The source offers a comparison corpus for textual rhythm across genres. Its stress assignments are analytical and it supplies no audio or listener responses. [Institutional full text](https://ah.lib.nccu.edu.tw/bitstream/140.119/62478/1/0125.pdf)

## 3. Psycholinguistics, neuroscience, and listening judgments

- **Established listening experiment.** Xiangbin Teng, Min Ma, Jinbiao Yang, Stefan Blohm, Qing Cai, and Xing Tian. 2020. “Constrained Structure of Ancient Chinese Poetry Facilitates Speech Content Grouping.” *Current Biology* 30(7), 1299–1305.e7. DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2020.01.059. Thirteen native Mandarin speakers heard 150 generated and 30 authentic five-character quatrains. The authors concatenated individually synthesized syllables at a fixed 300 ms, removing pauses and natural across-syllable intonation. Magnetoencephalography (MEG) showed grouping at the line timescale and changes on repeated exposure interpreted as prediction. Learned form can help listeners find lines even when conventional boundary cues are minimized. The task also elicited general poem-quality ratings that combine sound with other aspects of appraisal. Its synthetic, isochronous delivery limits conclusions about cold reading, expressive recitation, and natural pause behavior. The paper links its stimuli and analysis resources, whose redistribution terms require separate checking. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2020.01.059) · [Institutional full text](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3212740_6/component/file_3212741/content)
- **Established auditory ERP result in a controlled task.** Weijun Li (李卫君), Lin Wang, and Yufang Yang. 2014. “Chinese Tone and Vowel Processing Exhibits Distinctive Temporal Characteristics: An Electrophysiological Perspective from Classical Chinese Poem Processing.” *PLOS ONE* 9(1), e85683. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0085683. PMCID PMC3887106. Twenty Mandarin listeners judged meter while hearing unfamiliar quatrains read by one expert Mandarin speaker. The 160 experimental base quatrains supplied four conditions that varied the tone and/or vowel in the last syllable of the first couplet. Event-related potentials (ERPs) showed a right-hemisphere vowel-related negativity at 300–500 ms and later effects of tone and vowel at 600–1000 ms. The design supports making separate tone and rhyme-final perturbations. Cue timing varied naturally among conditions and the tone perturbation replaced tones 1/2 with tone 4, so the result cannot isolate a duration effect or generalize to every kind of 平仄 violation. The task measured metrical judgments. [Publisher full text](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0085683)
- **Established auditory ERP finding and noncausal group comparison.** Zhenghua Zhang (张政华), Mei Han, Fang Zhang, and Weijun Li. 2020. “Musical Training Improves Rhythm Integrative Processing of Classical Chinese Poem.” *Acta Psychologica Sinica* 52(7), 847–860. DOI 10.3724/SP.J.1041.2020.00847. Twenty-five musically trained and twenty-five untrained students heard unfamiliar seven-character quatrains with tone and/or vowel changes at the first couplet end. EEG analysis at the final syllable showed effects at 100–300 ms in the trained group and at 300–750 ms in both groups. This supports recording listener training and testing responses after a perturbation as well as at its location. Group assignment followed prior training, so causal claims about training are limited. The task assessed metrical appropriateness. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3724/SP.J.1041.2020.00847) · [Journal full text, author-checked English translation](https://journal.psych.ac.cn/acps/EN/article/downloadArticleFile.do?attachType=PDF&id=4740)
- **Established silent-reading ERP result.** Qingrong Chen (陈庆荣), Jingjing Zhang, Xiaodong Xu, Christoph Scheepers, Yiming Yang, and Michael K. Tanenhaus. 2016. “Prosodic Expectations in Silent Reading: ERP Evidence from Rhyme Scheme and Semantic Congruence in Classic Chinese Poems.” *Cognition* 154, 11–21. DOI 10.1016/j.cognition.2016.05.007. Nineteen Mandarin readers saw items from 148 seven-character Tang poems with factorial changes in rhyme and semantic congruity at the second-line final character. Rhyme affected P200 and a later right-lateralized response, and the semantic N400 difference was clearest when rhyme expectations were met. This supports comparing prosodic expectation and semantics as distinct predictors with a possible interaction. Presentation and measurement were visual and electrophysiological.
