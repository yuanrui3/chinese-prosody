# Data card · Historical rhyme-book lexicons

The Stage 3 historical tiers preserve the candidate readings attested for each character in the two rhyme-book sources. The annotation layer can select a candidate for a specific context when an explicit review supplies evidence. The two books are independent references. Their groups and labels are stored separately from the modern Mandarin and Cantonese lookup and from any actual pronunciation in audio.

## 《廣韻》 (Guangyun)

- **Source and edition.** 陳彭年 et al., 《大宋重修廣韻》 (1008), 澤存堂本 with corrections documented by the NK2028 maintainers. The digital transcription uses poem’s 廣韻字音表, updated phonological assignments and documented textual corrections. The selected repository revision is `21585e22c8a730ca2fd175112f4d18e16d5ce578`. [Pinned transcription](https://github.com/nk2028/tshet-uinh-data/blob/21585e22c8a730ca2fd175112f4d18e16d5ce578/%E9%9F%BB%E6%9B%B8/%E5%BB%A3%E9%9F%BB.csv) · [Source notes](https://github.com/nk2028/tshet-uinh-data/blob/21585e22c8a730ca2fd175112f4d18e16d5ce578/DEVELOP.md)
- **Rights.** The historical work is public domain. The NK2028 release applies CC0 1.0 Universal to its data. Cache `guangyun.csv` on Gilbreth. The loader checks its Git blob hash against the pinned source. [Upstream license](https://github.com/nk2028/tshet-uinh-data/blob/21585e22c8a730ca2fd175112f4d18e16d5ce578/LICENSE)
- **Processing.** Index the corrected headword and retain the transcribed headword, 小韻 and within-group entry IDs, gloss, and 反切. Unencoded character descriptions and deleted headwords are excluded from character lookup. Rows with identical phonological position (音韻地位) and original rhyme-group label (韻目原貌) within a headword share one candidate with all supporting rows. Tone and 平仄 come from the phonological position. The reference rhyme label comes from the transcription’s 韻目原貌 column. A phonological final family can differ from the book’s rhyme label.
- **Initial and final.** The loader adapts Ayaka’s Baxter 2014 transcription from the MIT-licensed NK2028 examples at commit `59142e340364947aa5089277185ba524775a670e`. It records the onset and final separately, with four historical tones in their own field. The transcription implements a Middle Chinese analysis. It gives a basis for comparing segmental structure without implying a recorded or acoustically calibrated reading. Where the position lacks a supported mapping, the transcription fields are null. [Pinned transcription algorithm](https://github.com/nk2028/tshet-uinh-examples/blob/59142e340364947aa5089277185ba524775a670e/baxter.js) · [Port license notice](LICENSE.baxter-port.txt)
- **Limits.** Entries represent the selected edition and its corrections. The historical scheme supplies a reference tier for texts from several periods. The character lexicon alone cannot determine which candidate applies to an occurrence in a passage.

## 《平水韻》 (Pingshui)

- **Source and edition.** The Chinese Wikisource 106-group compilation titled 《平水韻》, attributed there to 劉淵 and 王文鬱. The source is pinned at revision `8264804`, edited 2026-08-12. Coverage and character assignments refer to that online witness. [Pinned transcription](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=8264804&title=%E5%B9%B3%E6%B0%B4%E9%9F%BB)
- **Rights.** Wikisource marks the historical work public domain and provides site contributions under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribute Wikisource contributors and the pinned revision when sharing derived data. Apply CC BY-SA 4.0 where contributor rights attach. Cache the edition’s unconverted HTML as `pingshui.html` on Gilbreth. [Wikimedia terms of use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Processing.** The parser expects 106 populated groups in five volumes and keeps the complete group label, such as 上平聲一東 or 去聲一送. It includes the primary list for each group, excluding supplemental lines marked 詞 and 辭. Upper and lower 平 volumes share the 平 tone label. All group memberships for a character are retained. Since these lists supply group membership, the initial and final fields stay null for this tier.
- **Limits.** The lists give sensitivity checks on a later rhyme convention. A group is a textual category. Distances between sounds should use separate evidence from the historical transcription or performed audio.
