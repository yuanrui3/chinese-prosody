# Does a change of meaning at matched semicolon endings predict a written 7?

**Project hypothesis.** In 權德輿's 出於料外／盡落彀中, both phrases refer to the same military outcome. The first places it beyond expectation and the second inside the victor's grasp. Test the previously specified meaning-turn feature specifically in that position. Reserve this example and its author. [Original memorial](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=485359)

**Population and selection.** Screen attributed Tang works in 《全唐文》 bearing the formal titles 表、啟、箋、牋、書、序 or 檄. A sentence must have exactly one internal semicolon and a supporting phrase before the ending on **each** side. The two final punctuation-delimited phrases must be distinct and contain the same number of Han characters, at least three. Thus the two complete four-character phrases in the motivating example qualify. Two isolated two-character tails or an adjacent pair elsewhere do not qualify. Preserve the edition's punctuation. Exclude damaged or mixed-verse witnesses, source-marked quotations, duplicate target sentences and text already encountered in the project. Reserve the previously used authors and titles, including candidates fetched for the preceding mixed-position study. Include one work per credited author. The identifiable population is parallel passages in formal Tang prose.

Draw works without semantic flags, predictions or reference grades. Use the first 180 volumes in a coprime stride through volumes 120–879, starting at 157 and stepping by 137. Visit the works in catalogue order within each volume and interleave volumes by title rank. Stop after ten distinct authors with four eligible targets, or after 2,000 eligible-genre titles or exhaustion of this frame. At that cap, use additional distinct authors in discovery order with at least two usable targets. Take at most eight targets per work, at evenly spaced midpoint ranks. Never fill a shortfall with adjacent phrases. Keep complete work context for the baseline. Retain revisions and a rejection log on Gilbreth. Freshness means held out from identifiable project data. The training corpora of the language models cannot be independently audited.

**Reference and masking.** Score the whole unmarked original sentence in its paragraph on the project's 1–7 written musicality (音律美) rubric. A 6 has a strong, sustained cadence. Reserve 7 for a line worth memorizing for the sound and phrasing of its particular wording. Use the full scale without a quota. Record an exact span and a brief reason for each rating. The editorial reader is the coordinating AI, which knows the research question. Withhold named sources, the classifier's flags, foil identities and all model forecasts from that reading. Seal grades before opening predictions. These are editorial judgments. No author, participant or listening grades are collected.

**Fixed comparisons.** Reuse the prior Qwen classifier verbatim with both prompt orders, its eight diagnostic examples, and the frozen prototype from the 《哀江南賦》 checkpoint. Keep the previously specified bonus of 0.75, capped at 7, with a 6.50 cutoff for a predicted 7. Compare it with the current prototype, always giving 6, and the existing literal opposition control. Use one same-width second-phrase replacement from another sentence of the same work where available. Shuffle flags 999 times within work. Report the inclusive one-sided tail after adding one to count and denominator. Do not tune on these works.

**Decision.** Pair every reference 7 with every 6 within each work. Count a correctly ordered pair as one, a tie as one half, and average the resulting proportions equally across works containing both grades. Also report pooled wins, ties and inversions, false 7s on reference 6s, correct 7 calls, mean absolute error averaged equally across all works and grades, and sensitivity to dropping each work. As in the preceding experiment, adopt only if ordering beats both comparators, absolute error is no larger than either, and at least two works contribute comparisons. This study measures written 音律美. Perceived musicality in a recording and read-aloud ease (顺口) require their own observations.
