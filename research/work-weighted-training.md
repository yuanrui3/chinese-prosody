# Giving each training work equal weight

**Question.** Does redistributing the training weight widen the written musical beauty (音律美) estimates and identify more of the sentences an editorial reader gives a 7? The earlier model's 80 forecasts lay between 5.02 and 6.39. One work, 《拉法叶赋》, supplied 209 of its 337 training units. The earlier sample is a retrospective check only. **Single change.** Keep the released full model's training examples, features, historical dictionaries, ridge penalty of 10, paragraph context, and output bounds of 1–7. Give each of the seven works total weight 337/7 and each sentence within a work the same share. Use these weights in computing the means, scales, intercept and regression. Compare with the released sentence-weighted model and the constant 6. Freeze all predictions and keep the existing unrounded cutoff of 6.50 for calling a 7.

**Fresh test.** Use Tang regulated rhapsodies (律賦) for parallel prose (駢文) and period-matched discursive prose. Reserve earlier works and authors, including the earlier discarded source screen. Match the two arms on documented periods of author activity. Select six distinct, complete sentences at spaced positions in each work, independently of scores. Give the predictors complete paragraphs. Two separate AI editorial sessions receive an anonymous packet containing only the target, up to two preceding and two following sentences from the same paragraph, and the established written 1–7 rubric. Designate the primary session in advance. Ask for one exact target quotation and a specific reason for every judgment. The second reading tests sensitivity to the reader.

**Outcomes and decision fixed before collection.** Report the range and standard deviation (SD) of bounded, unrounded scores, with equal weight per test work, and the mean of the within-work SDs. At 6.50 count true 7s caught, true 7s missed, and false 7s, including false calls on grades of exactly 6. Report precision, recall and mean absolute error (MAE). Use paired work resampling within genres for uncertainty and report the number of draws in which rates are defined. Shuffle the 7 labels only within works to check whether any calls match the actual target positions.

Advance this candidate for further validation only if the primary reading has larger overall and average within-work SD than the released model, more correct 7s than both comparators, newly caught 7s in at least two works, no increase in the count of false 7s versus the released model, and MAE no higher than either comparator. Describe each outcome even if the combined criteria fail. A wider range alone does not validate the score.
