# Can the model rank an exceptional sentence above a good one in the same work?

**Project hypothesis.** A model trained on differences between sentences in the same work will rank a written musical beauty (音律美) grade of 7 above a 5 or 6 more often than the released model. The preceding experiment found an ordering problem even when both editorial readers agreed on which sentences deserved 7. Its sixteen works are reserved. [Prior results](../reports/work-weighted-training.md)

**Training.** Reuse the frozen 337 editorial grades from seven training works. Within each work, compare every grade 7 with every grade 5 or 6. Fit a linear paired-comparison model to the difference in the two sentences’ features, using the release’s training means, scales and missing-value handling. Give each informative work equal total influence and every eligible pair within it an equal share. Minimize weighted mean logistic loss plus 0.05 times the squared coefficient norm. There is no intercept in a difference. Keep the same historical tone, rhyme, structure and grammatical features as the released predictor. Freeze the fitted coefficients, source revisions, context and forecasts before either reader begins. No threshold or 1–7 calibration is needed for an ordering.

**Fresh sample.** Select eight Tang regulated rhapsodies (律賦), a form of parallel prose (駢文), and eight works of discursive prose matched by broad period of author activity. Use different credited authors, pinned editions, intact passages and the existing source-exposure inventory. Choose eight distinct complete targets at spaced positions per work without reference to features or grades. Preserve their full paragraphs for prediction. Two separate AI editorial sessions each grade the same 128 anonymous sentences independently on the existing written 1–7 rubric. Each can see up to two sentences of same-paragraph context on either side. The primary reader is designated before release. There are no grade quotas and no extra sampling after grades become known.

**Primary comparison.** For every pair in the same fresh work whose primary grades are 7 and 5 or 6, award one point if the model puts the 7 above its counterpart, half a point for a tie, and zero otherwise. Average first within each informative work, then equally across those works. Compare the new ranking with the current unrounded score and the constant 6, which ties every pair. Report how many works and pairs qualify, 7 versus 5 and 7 versus 6 separately, and both genres. Mark a comparison with zero eligible works as undefined. The pairs share sentences, so uncertainty resamples whole works, paired across predictors and stratified by genre (10,000 draws). Shuffle complete grade assignments within each work 999 times. A supplementary uncertainty check preserves intact chronological matches. Repeat on the second reader's grades, retaining that reader’s own denominators.

**Decision.** Advance the candidate to further written validation only if the primary work-resampled 95% lower bound on its advantage exceeds zero against both comparators, with at least two informative works in each genre. Treat the reader replication, subgroups and shuffled controls as sensitivity checks. A rank measures relative order within a work. Its scale cannot supply a 1–7 grade or compare absolute quality across works. Keep listening and first-read ease (顺口) as separate final participant outcomes.
