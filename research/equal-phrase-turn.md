# Does a turn in meaning across equal-length phrases help identify a written 7?

**Project hypothesis.** In 權德輿, 出於料外 and 盡落彀中 refer to the same military outcome. An apparent limit to prediction gives way to completed capture. Equal-length phrases may carry special force when the second reinterprets the first by reversing an expectation, direction, status or perspective on a shared matter. A second image, a lexical opposite or a matching construction alone supplies insufficient evidence. The motivating memorial, all its author's works, and the earlier Yu Xin transfer sources and candidates are reserved from the test. [Motivating witness](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=485359)

**Target selection before outcomes.** Use fresh credited authors from the formal prose of 《全唐文》. Select at most one work per author. Search predeclared volume order starting at 200 with stride 11; prioritize memorials (表) and formal letters (啟), then additional formal prose genres as needed. Exclude mixed verse, prior authors and sources, and earlier normalized passage matches. Prefer eight works with eight eligible sentences apiece. If necessary, complete the sample with works yielding six or seven eligible sentences. Require at least six independent authors. Draw each work's targets at evenly spaced midpoint ranks through eligible sentences in edition order. Keep complete paragraph context. Report the resulting genre mix and selection limits.

**Single pair per target.** Within each complete sentence, segment on Chinese and ASCII commas, enumeration commas, colons, semicolons and final punctuation. The two phrases must have the same count of at least three Han characters after NFKC normalization, and different normalized text. If there is exactly one internal semicolon, try the final phrase on each side first. Otherwise, or when those closings do not qualify, take the last eligible adjacent pair. Preserve the full sentence for classification and the full source paragraphs for grading and baseline predictions. This fixes the opportunity to earn the bonus at one pair per sentence. All tested sentences already contain equal length.

**Independent judgments.** A fresh AI editorial reading of the anonymous text assigns one written 音律美 rating to each selected sentence, with 7 reserved for a memorization recommendation. Its instructions omit the pair highlights, project hypothesis and predictions. The primary feature classifier is a frozen local Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with two differently worded prompts and reversed binary choice order. A turn requires both passes to endorse it. The classifier sees a whole sentence with two marked phrases and no grades or author metadata. Report each vote, consensus, and predeclared positive and negative examples, including the motivating passage. These votes share model weights.

A second, independently prompted AI annotator classifies all authentic pairs and up to twenty deterministic same-width replacements, mixed with opaque IDs and without model outputs, aesthetic scores or source metadata. It records the shared matter and the specific shift when endorsing a turn. Use its flags as a prespecified **secondary** measurement of the same feature. Report agreement with Qwen, disagreements, and results with those flags. This is a text-based model audit. No participant is involved.

**Comparisons and controls.** For the candidate add 0.75 to the frozen contextual grammar prediction on a positive flag, capped at 7. Use the existing 6.50 threshold. Compare the current model, always predicting 6, and the same bonus on a spelling cue instead. The spelling cue looks for opposing members of one of the following pairs across the final two Han characters of each selected phrase, in either order: 外內、外中、前後、始終、有無、得失、去來、進退、盛衰、榮辱、存亡、生死、開閉、高下、遠近、憂喜、成敗、古今、彼此、難易、上下. It is a literal lookup, with no sense disambiguation.

For a textual control, replace the second phrase by a same-Han-width phrase from a different sentence of the same work. Exclude phrases already in the target. Prefer the same paragraph and take the lowest SHA-256 of the seeded target/donor/phrase key. Score the unmarked parts of the sentence as original context. Keep targets without a donor in the score comparison. For a statistical control, shuffle the consensus flags within work and pair route (semicolon closings or adjacent) across marked sentences 999 times with seed 20261125. Report the inclusive one-sided tail with plus-one correction.

**Primary endpoint and decision.** Order every editorial 7 against every 6 from the same work using raw forecasts, count exact forecast ties as half, then average equally across informative works. Also report the number of informative works and pairs, pooled correct/inverted/tied counts, leave-one-work-out sensitivity, work-weighted mean absolute error across all scores, sensitivity at 6.50 and false 7s. Publish the rates of feature matches separately for 7s and 6s. A claimed improvement requires better primary ordering than both baselines, no larger all-grade error than either, and at least two informative works. If there are no usable comparisons, report that directly. Do not tune thresholds or choose a winning annotator after the fact. The endpoint is written editorial 音律美. Heard 音律美 and first-read ease (顺口) have independent final-stage participant protocols.
