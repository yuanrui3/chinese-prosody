# Can the written scorer rank two historical readings of the same passage?

**Result.** In six fresh paired passages where the released scorer could represent both historical readings, changing the pronunciation produced six exact ties. With the surrounding lines of 陸機’s 《大暮賦》 included, the scorer recognized the rhyme favored by 顏師古 and ranked that reading slightly lower. The frozen model gives a small negative weight to the specific cross-line rhyme feature that changed.

Both readings of 貰 have departing tone (去聲), with different rhyme groups. The two 渾 targets and 寰 fall at odd, nonfinal positions in their groups. The model measures tone alternation on even syllables and compares rhyme at endings. Always giving 6 also tied all six passages. Swapping the two condition labels in each possible combination gave six ties in all 64 assignments. The two 復 passages share one historical dispute, and the two 渾 passages share another. **What the surrounding rhyme changed.** In 《大暮賦》 the endings of three consecutive paired lines are **逝、貰、勵**. The pinned 《廣韻》 assigns 逝 and 勵 to 祭. Reading 貰 as 勢 makes it match both neighbors. Reading it as 時夜反 matches neither. The model’s cross-line rhyme measure therefore rose from **0 to 1**. Every other model feature held steady, but the estimate fell from **5.890 to 5.831**. In the fitted model, moving this feature from 0 to 1 costs 0.0597 points with other features fixed. This explains the direction on this passage. The small, source-selected sample cannot establish a generally preferable rhyme weight. In the fuller 《魏都賦》 passage, the two 寰 readings again tied at **5.820**. ## What the critics judged

**Regulated parallel rhapsodies (律賦).** 孫奕 quotes two entries on the same examination subject, one by 熊淳 and one by 沈之萬. 林德頌, as quoted by 孫, treats the departing reading of 復 as the original reading. 孫 distinguishes restoring an earlier practice from doing something again and explicitly approves the entering tone for the former. In another exchange 林 prescribes rising-tone 渾 for the instrument 渾天儀. He rejects 葉重開’s level reading and approves 姚鳳’s use. 孫 cites both readings and accepts either. These are four passages, two shared disputes, and two distinct critics. [孫奕, 《示兒編》卷九, 雙音字貴詳審](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=749881)

**Rhyme instructions in earlier paired prose.** In the surviving quotation from 陸機’s 《大暮賦》, 顏師古 argues for 貰 read like 勢 from the neighboring endings 逝 and 勵. The comparison uses the scholarly alternative 時夜反, which he explicitly rejects. For 左思’s 寰 he rejects reading the word as 環 and interprets it as the historical spelling of administrative 縣. The specific departing-tone candidate is a project mapping from that interpretation. 李善 specifies a reading to fit the rhyme for 潘岳’s 錯 and 郭璞’s 嶠. Both works had already appeared in Stage 7. [顏師古 on 貰](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=525586) · [顏師古 on 寰](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=754111) · [李善 on 錯](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2013069) · [李善 on 嶠](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2009083)

**Coverage of the historical dictionary.** 焦竑 cites the two questions in 顏之推’s 《稽聖賦》 to defend the entering-tone 廿 and rejects the popular reading like 念. 顏師古 reads 陸機’s 斡 like 管 and connects the rejected sound to confusion with 捾. Both pairs require a cited sound-alike for one condition. 李善 gives two complete fanqie (反切) spellings for 張協’s 殼 and 陸機’s 揥. Neither pair is available in full under the written graph in the project’s 《廣韻》. The separate calculation supplies only the supported tone and rhyme. For 揥 the preferred 去聲霽韻 and the native 去聲祭韻 are different categories. ## Scoring and interpretation

The 14-case main packet was sealed before scoring. The additional case was sealed separately before inspecting the main scores. The experiment uses the previously trained written 音律美 model. Each comparison holds the Chinese characters, punctuation, span, other dictionary entries and model weights fixed. The principal score uses the entire paired excerpt as a single input. Where the witness preserves neighboring lines, a second run includes those lines in both conditions. For 寰 and 廿 this second presentation also changes the extent of the target unit. The no-information comparator gives both readings 6. Individual source judgements concern rhyme, pronunciation, or meaning. They supply a direction for this comparison and cannot establish a population’s aesthetic preference. A Tang catalogue contains an unrelated work with 葉重開’s exam title. Other lines of 《文賦》 had appeared in an acoustic benchmark. Freshness refers to the project’s earlier evaluations. It is not an assessment of external model pretraining.
