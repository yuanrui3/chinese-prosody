# 顺口 / 音律美 research

- Follow the seven stages in the research brief. Record the outcome in `reports/stage-N.md` at each stage boundary. Bring decisions about the reference reading and genre scope to the author.
- Write in English and give Chinese terminology in characters on first use. Cite primary sources read and label interpretations as established findings, contested claims, or project hypotheses. Keep unread and inaccessible references under `UNVERIFIED`.
- Audio is central. Preserve speaker, style, variety, text edition, and recording rights in corpus manifests. Use public-domain or openly licensed text, and only use audio with documented reuse rights or permission. Treat synthetic audio as experimental stimuli.
- Keep corpus downloads, model caches, intermediate audio, and GPU outputs on Gilbreth. Use Slurm for GPU and other intensive work. See [Gilbreth instructions](hpc/README.md).
- Preserve ambiguous readings in annotations. Validate each text metric against controlled and shuffled data and compare it to its acoustic counterpart when available.
- Stage 1 deliverables are the [bibliography](research/literature.md), [evidence synthesis](research/synthesis.md), and [checkpoint report](reports/stage-1.md). Run `bash -n hpc/gpu-smoke.sbatch` to check the batch script. Add the pinned environment and `make all` when the metric and figure pipeline exists.
- Stage 2 deliverables are the [mathematical foundations](theory/foundations.md) and [checkpoint report](reports/stage-2.md). Start with 骈文 and a period-matched prose comparison. Use historical categories for text and actual pronunciation for recordings. Store critical judgments with exact spans and provenance. Keep the production and listening outcomes independent during validation.
- Stage 3 uses `bash hpc/stage3-pilot.sh "$SHUNKOU_ROOT"` to build the small [matched prose pilot](data/pilot/DATA_CARD.md) with pinned witnesses and lexical annotations. See the [Stage 3 checkpoint](reports/stage-3.md) for measured coverage and the [collection cards](data/collections/DATA_CARDS.md) for the expansion importers. Keep generated data and audio in the Gilbreth project directory.
- Stage 4 uses [one GPU on Gilbreth](hpc/README.md#stage-4-audio-through-the-gateway) for the controlled recordings and waveform-first alignment. See the [audio data card](audio/README.md) and [Stage 4 checkpoint](reports/stage-4.md). Preserve intended phonemes, acoustic drafts, verified performed pronunciations, and historical text categories separately.
- Stage 5 uses `python -m unittest discover -s tests` for local fixtures and the [Gateway Slurm runner](hpc/README.md#stage-5-validation-through-the-gateway) for the live corpus check and seeded pilot controls. The [checkpoint](reports/stage-5.md) records matched prose and synthetic-audio results. Keep generated results with the project data on Gilbreth.
- Stage 6 keeps the [blind listening survey and cold read-aloud task](reports/stage-6.md) independent. Use the [Gateway instructions](hpc/README.md#stage-6-pilot-through-the-gateway) and the [production protocol](audio/STAGE6_PRODUCTION.md). Keep keys, audio, and individual responses on Gilbreth.
- Stage 7 adds `./shunkou score TEXT`, [Gateway waveform scoring](hpc/README.md#stage-7-scoring-through-the-gateway), the [release checkpoint](reports/stage-7.md), and the [final report](reports/final.md). Run `make all` for the tests, shell syntax checks, and figures. Use the pinned Stage 4 environment for waveform scoring. Keep rendered audio HTML on Gilbreth.
