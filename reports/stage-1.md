**Established.** Historical rhyme and tonal categories give computable descriptions of form. Mandarin listeners detect changes in those cues, and human studies of chanting and recitation demonstrate measurable differences in phrasing and duration. The LibriVox catalogue and U.S. public-domain status were checked for a multilingual Tang-poetry pilot. **Project hypothesis.** Some text metrics will predict where readers pause or hesitate, and those effects may differ by variety, delivery style, and genre. A study with both first-read recordings and blind listening judgments can test whether ease of saying and pleasure in hearing move together.
