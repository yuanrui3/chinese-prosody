# Does neighboring rhyme improve the written musicality estimate?

**Prospective decision.** Compare unrounded 1–7 predictions against a fresh AI editorial reading of written musical beauty (音律美). Fit the existing grammar and context model on its original 337 scored units. Fit it again with both neighboring-rhyme columns removed, keeping within-sentence rhyme, historical tone, grammar and structure. Compare both with a constant 6. A fourth, descriptive fit removes both the neighboring and the within-sentence rhyme columns. Use original work contexts in every fit.

**Sampling and masking.** Select eight Tang regulated rhapsodies (律賦) and eight Tang formal prose works from different authors. Exclude the prior masked-rhyme works and authors. Sample five complete sentences spaced across each work before looking at any score. Keep all original sentences for the model. Show each editorial reader the target with up to two preceding and two following sentences from the same paragraph. Hide the title, author, assigned rhyme, source class, hypothesis and forecasts. Textual clues can still reveal genre or a familiar sentence. Two separate AI sessions receive the same rubric and packet, with independent responses. Designate the first reader as primary before either returns. Give no quotas or feedback. Each reader supplies an integer grade, a short quote from the target and a specific reason for every grade.

**Inference.** Average absolute error within each work and then equally over the sixteen works. Report results by genre. Resample whole works within genre with paired predictions and 10,000 draws. Retain neighboring rhyme as a supported numerical contribution only if the full model's error is lower than both the model without neighboring rhyme and always giving 6, with both upper 95% bounds below zero. Estimate coefficient sensitivity by resampling training works and by omitting each training work in turn. The second editorial reading and a replay of sealed earlier ratings are sensitivity analyses. Neither can override the primary decision. Keep published scores frozen during this experiment.

Historical readings are dictionary categories for the written features. Heard musical beauty and first-read ease (顺口) keep their distinct final-stage participant tests.

**Period check added before ratings.** The initial acquisition admitted Wang Pu’s (王樸) Later Zhou calendar memorial and Xu Xuan’s (徐鉉) memorial on the Southern Tang emperor. The anthology includes Five Dynasties works, and a page marked 唐 was insufficient evidence. The source packet and its unopened automated forecasts were set aside. For the primary packet require that the anthology prints the work under the credited author’s own heading, that the author notice gives a Tang reign or court marker through the reign of Xuanzong (宣宗), and that the notice contains none of the specified counterindications. Restrict the source volumes to 120–799 and retain the biographical citation for each work. The author’s dated activity establishes the period match at dynasty level.
